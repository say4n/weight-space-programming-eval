Design: I hand-build row-wise features. The first layer detects, for each of the 8 playable rows, whether the player, an enemy, or gold is present, plus a signed gold-vs-player column difference. The second layer gates the column difference by the current row to produce “gold is left/right in my row,” and builds per-row vertical preferences: move up/down to escape an enemy in the current row if the target row is safe, or to align with gold above/below when the current row is safe. The output layer sums these row-wise votes with biases that make no-op the default and strongly suppress fire.
