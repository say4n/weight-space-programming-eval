Design: a hand-coded reactive policy. First-layer units compute player/diver coordinates, diver-presence, oxygen/diver gauge indicators, wall/row threat detectors, and bullet indicators. Second-layer units form gated directional features for chasing divers and for surfacing only when oxygen is low and a diver is actually carried. The final layer turns these features into action logits: chase divers when they are visible, otherwise drift downward to search; dodge vertically when an enemy is in the current row; turn/fire opportunistically; and ascend to surface when the gauge indicates both low oxygen and a carried diver.
