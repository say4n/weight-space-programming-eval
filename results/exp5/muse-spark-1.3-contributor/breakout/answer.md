My policy shadows the ball's x. Normally it drives the paddle toward the ball's next x `2*b-tx` to stay aligned without lag; when the ball is in row 8 (about to hit the paddle) it drives toward the current `b` to get a centre hit and preserve x-direction. h1 computes 5 primitives: is-y8, b-p, p-b, 2b-tx-p, p-2b+tx. h2 computes 4 ANDs: y8&C1, y8&C2, ~y8&N1, ~y8&N2. Outputs sum them: right = A1+A3, left = A2+A4, nop = -(sum), with large biases so exactly one wins by ~20.
